# Roadmap

Merels is a nine men's morris game in the browser. Two players place nine pieces each on the 24-point board, take a piece after a mill, slide to an empty neighbour, fly with three pieces, win when the other colour cannot play, mark legal points, and undo a ply. About 35 hours remain.

## Later work

These stay as later work. They are about 35 hours.

### Opening book (4 hours)

Suggest a first-place reply from a small set of known openings.

### Difficulty (3 hours)

Offer an easy or a medium opponent that looks one mill ahead.

### Clocks (3 hours)

An optional timer on each colour's turn.

### Capture animation (3 hours)

Lift a taken piece off the board instead of clearing it at once.

### Save and load (3 hours)

Write a position to a local file and restore it.

### Match of three (3 hours)

Play until one colour wins two games, and keep that tally.

### Print diagram (3 hours)

Lay the board and the move list out for one printed page.

### Six men's morris (3 hours)

A smaller inner-and-middle board with six pieces each.

### Twelve men's morris (4 hours)

Add the two diagonal lines and twelve pieces each.

### Keyboard (3 hours)

Move a cursor between points with the arrows and place with Enter.

### Drag pieces (3 hours)

Drag a piece to an empty point during the sliding phase.

An opening book is the next piece. The game can place, mill, slide, fly, win, hint, and undo, and it does not yet suggest an opening reply.
